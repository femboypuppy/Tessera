On **June 16, 1963**, Valentina Tereshkova became the first woman in space. Her Vostok 6 flight lasted almost three days and made 48 orbits of Earth: on that one flight she logged more time in space than all the American astronauts who had flown before her, combined.

She was a textile factory worker and an amateur skydiver, chosen from more than 400 applicants. Parachuting mattered: like [[Yuri Gagarin]], she left her capsule in the air and landed under her own parachute.

Her call sign was *Chaika*, "seagull."

> [!info] Twenty years
> No other woman flew in space until Svetlana Savitskaya in 1982. Sally Ride became the first American woman in space in 1983, on the [[Space Shuttle]].

Tereshkova later became a politician and remains the only woman to have flown a solo space mission.

Part of the [[Space Race]].
