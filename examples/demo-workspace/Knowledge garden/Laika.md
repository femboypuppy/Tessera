# Laika

Laika, a stray dog from the streets of Moscow, was the first animal to orbit Earth. She flew on
**Sputnik 2**, launched on **November 3, 1957**, less than a month after [[Sputnik 1]].

Sputnik 2 was built in about four weeks, at the political leadership's request, to mark the
anniversary of the October Revolution. There was no way to bring it back to Earth.

For decades, Soviet officials said Laika lived for several days in orbit. In 2002, a scientist
who had worked on the mission revealed that she died within hours of launch, from overheating.

> [!warning] A hard legacy
> Laika's flight showed that a living creature could survive launch and weightlessness, which
> made [[Yuri Gagarin]]'s flight possible. It also made many people ask what we owe the animals
> we send first. A monument to her stands near the facility in Moscow where she was trained.

Later Soviet dogs, such as Belka and Strelka in 1960, came home safely.

Part of the [[Space Race]].
