# Mars rovers

Robotic vehicles that drive across the surface of Mars. Each generation has been bigger, smarter
and longer-lived than the last.

| Rover           | Landed            | Size          | Known for                                                        |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sojourner       | July 4, 1997      | Microwave oven | The first rover on another planet, on the Mars Pathfinder mission. |
| Spirit          | January 4, 2004   | Golf cart     | Planned for 90 Martian days, it worked for more than six years.  |
| Opportunity     | January 25, 2004  | Golf cart     | Also planned for 90 days, it worked for 14 years and drove over 45 km, farther than a marathon. |
| Curiosity       | August 6, 2012    | Small car     | Lowered on cables by a "sky crane"; found that Gale crater once held a lake. |
| Perseverance    | February 18, 2021 | Small car     | Collects rock samples for a future return to Earth; brought the Ingenuity helicopter. |

China's Zhurong rover also landed, in May 2021, making China the second country to operate a rover
on Mars.

## Ingenuity

On April 19, 2021, the small helicopter Ingenuity made the first powered, controlled flight on
another planet. It was planned as a five-flight experiment and flew 72 times.

> [!info] Launch windows
> Most of these rovers launched about 26 months apart, when Earth and Mars line up for the
> cheapest path. See [[Hohmann transfer orbit]].

## Messages from Mars

Opportunity's last message came in June 2018, during a planet-wide dust storm that covered its
solar panels. NASA ended the mission in February 2019.

Curiosity and Perseverance run on nuclear power and keep driving.
