Mir ("peace" or "world" in Russian) was a modular space station in low Earth orbit from **1986 to 2001**. It was the first station assembled in orbit from several modules, launched one by one over ten years, the approach the [[International Space Station]] later followed.

## Records

- Valeri Polyakov spent 437 consecutive days aboard in 1994–1995, still the longest single spaceflight.
- It hosted more than a hundred visitors from a dozen countries.

## Shuttle–Mir

From 1995 to 1998 the [[Space Shuttle]] docked with Mir nine times, and seven American astronauts lived aboard for long stays. Two former rivals of the [[Space Race]] learned to work together, which became the foundation of the ISS partnership.

> [!warning] 1997: a hard year
> A fire broke out on board in February 1997, and in June a cargo ship collided with the Spektr module and punctured it. The crew sealed off the module and saved the station.

## The end

Mir was deliberately brought down on March 23, 2001, over the South Pacific.
