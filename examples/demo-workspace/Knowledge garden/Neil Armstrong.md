# Neil Armstrong

Neil Armstrong (1930–2012) was the first person to walk on the Moon, as commander of
[[Apollo 11]].

## Before Apollo

- Born on August 5, 1930, in Wapakoneta, Ohio. He had a pilot's license before a driver's
  license.
- Flew 78 combat missions as a Navy pilot in the Korean War.
- As a test pilot, flew the X-15 rocket plane to the edge of space.
- Commanded Gemini 8 in 1966: the first docking of two spacecraft, and an emergency he solved in
  seconds when the spacecraft began to spin. See [[Project Gemini]].

## The first step

On July 21, 1969 (UTC), he stepped off *Eagle*'s footpad onto the Moon. Most photos from the
surface show Buzz Aldrin, because Armstrong carried the camera for most of the moonwalk.

## After

He never flew in space again. He taught aerospace engineering at the University of Cincinnati,
served on the commission that investigated the Challenger accident (see [[Space Shuttle]]), and
avoided fame for the rest of his life. He died on August 25, 2012.
