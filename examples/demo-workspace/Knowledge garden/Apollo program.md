# Apollo program

The Apollo program (1961–1972) landed twelve people on the Moon and brought them all home. It
answered President Kennedy's 1961 challenge to land a man on the Moon before the end of the decade,
with five months to spare.

## The spacecraft

- The **command and service module** carried three astronauts to lunar orbit and back.
- The **lunar module** carried two of them down to the surface and back up.
- The **[[Saturn V]]** launched it all, designed by [[Wernher von Braun]]'s team.

## The missions that mattered

| Mission    | When           | Why it matters                                                   |
| ---------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Apollo 1   | January 1967   | A cabin fire during a ground test killed Gus Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee. The spacecraft was redesigned. |
| Apollo 8   | December 1968  | First humans to leave Earth orbit and circle the Moon; the *Earthrise* photograph. |
| [[Apollo 11]] | July 1969   | First landing: [[Neil Armstrong]] and Buzz Aldrin.               |
| Apollo 12  | November 1969  | A precision landing next to the Surveyor 3 probe.                |
| [[Apollo 13]] | April 1970  | An explosion on the way out; the crew came home safely.          |
| Apollo 15  | July 1971      | The first lunar rover.                                           |
| Apollo 17  | December 1972  | The last landing, and the only geologist on the Moon, Harrison Schmitt. |

Six missions landed (11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17) and brought back 382 kg of rock and soil, which
scientists still study today.

## The people on the ground

About 400,000 people worked on Apollo, from seamstresses sewing spacesuits to mathematicians like
[[Katherine Johnson]], who checked the trajectories. Getting there took the skills learned in
[[Project Mercury]] and [[Project Gemini]].

> [!info] Christmas Eve, 1968
> While orbiting the Moon, the Apollo 8 crew read from the Book of Genesis on live television. It
> was the most-watched broadcast in history up to then.

Nobody has been back since 1972. The [[Artemis program]] plans to change that.
