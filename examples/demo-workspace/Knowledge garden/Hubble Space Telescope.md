Hubble has orbited Earth since **1990**, above the blur of the atmosphere. It is one of the most productive scientific instruments ever built.

## A shaky start

The [[Space Shuttle]] Discovery launched it on April 24, 1990. Within weeks, astronomers saw that its images were blurry: the 2.4 m main mirror had been ground to the wrong shape, off by about 2 micrometers at the edges, a fiftieth of the width of a human hair.

> [!success] Glasses for a telescope
> In December 1993 astronauts installed corrective optics on the first servicing mission. The images became sharp. Four more servicing missions followed, the last in 2009.

## What Hubble showed us

- **The Hubble Deep Field (1995):** ten days staring at an apparently empty patch of sky revealed about 3,000 galaxies.
- **The expansion of the universe:** Hubble helped measure how fast it expands, and observations of distant supernovae with ground telescopes and Hubble showed that the expansion is speeding up.
- **Pillars of Creation (1995):** one of the most famous images in astronomy.

## Hubble and Webb

The [[James Webb Space Telescope]] is not its replacement but its partner: Hubble sees mostly visible and ultraviolet light, Webb sees infrared.
