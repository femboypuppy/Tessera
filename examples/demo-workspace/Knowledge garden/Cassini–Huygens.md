A mission to Saturn by NASA, the European Space Agency and the Italian Space Agency. It launched on **October 15, 1997**, and after [[Gravity assist|gravity assists]] from Venus (twice), Earth and Jupiter, entered orbit around Saturn on **July 1, 2004** (UTC).

## Huygens lands on Titan

ESA's Huygens probe separated from Cassini and landed on Saturn's largest moon, Titan, on **January 14, 2005**. It is still the most distant landing ever made. Titan turned out to have rivers, lakes and rain, but of liquid methane and ethane instead of water.

## Thirteen years at Saturn

- Found jets of water vapor and ice erupting from the moon Enceladus, from an ocean under its ice shell. That ocean is now one of the best places to look for life beyond Earth.
- Watched storms, seasons and the hexagon-shaped jet stream at Saturn's north pole.
- Mapped the rings in extraordinary detail.

## The Grand Finale

Running out of fuel, Cassini made 22 dives between Saturn and its rings, then plunged into the planet on **September 15, 2017**, sending data until the end. It was destroyed on purpose, so it could never crash into and contaminate Enceladus or Titan.

> [!note] Following Voyager
> [[Voyager program|Voyager 1]] flew past Titan in 1980 but couldn't see through its haze. Cassini and Huygens finally did.
